- The reminder email names the visit’s start and end in that location’s timezone, with the abbreviation, and includes the short booking reference. A visit that crosses a daylight-saving change names both, such as Sat, Mar 7, 10:00 AM EST – Mon, Mar 9, 11:00 AM EDT. The location zone wins over the business zone. A location stored as UTC still shows UTC. A location zone that is not a real name uses the business zone. Another workspace’s booking is not named. The subject stays “Booking reminder.” The price, payments, taxes, and fees stay as they were. The time line wraps on a 390px-wide screen. Spec Section 2.4.
