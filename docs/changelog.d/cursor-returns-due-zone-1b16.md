- `/staff/returns` names each rental’s due time in that location’s timezone, with the abbreviation. On the fall-back morning, 1:30 AM EDT and 1:30 AM EST stay distinct. A due time on the spring-forward morning uses the abbreviation for that instant, so 1:30 AM EST and 3:30 AM EDT do not share a blank clock. A rental that is still today in Los Angeles and already tomorrow in New York stays on the list only for Los Angeles. The location zone wins over the business zone. A location stored as UTC still shows UTC. A location zone that is not a real name uses the business zone. Another workspace’s rental is not listed. Opening the page does not write a price, a payment, a tax, a fee, a refund, or an audit row. The return-time field and the late fee stay as they were. Stored instants stay UTC. The due line wraps on a 390px-wide screen. Spec Section 2.4.
