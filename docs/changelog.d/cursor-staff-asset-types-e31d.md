- An owner or manager adds an asset type at `/staff/setup/resources` and assigns it to a boat or a counted pool. The name is unique in that workspace. A service option can use that boat or pool once it has a type. Staff can see the type and cannot change it. A member limited to other locations, and another workspace, cannot assign it. Saving does not write a price, a payment, a tax, a fee, or a refund. The type controls wrap on a 390px-wide screen. Spec Section 4.1.
