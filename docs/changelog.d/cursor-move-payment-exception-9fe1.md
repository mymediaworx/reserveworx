- Staff move a booking that was paid after its time was taken onto a free time. The booking page shows Change time for a `payment_exception` rental or tour and does not show Cancel, extend, or no-show. A free time becomes confirmed, writes the allocation, and queues the confirmation once. A time that is taken leaves the booking as a payment exception and writes no allocation. Moving it again after it is confirmed does not queue a second confirmation. Another workspace is not found. Staff who can only view bookings cannot move it. The price, payments, taxes, fees, and refunds stay as they were. The notice and the time fields wrap on a 390px-wide screen. Spec Section 14 item 12.
