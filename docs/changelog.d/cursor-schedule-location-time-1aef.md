On the fall-back morning, 1:00 AM EDT and 1:00 AM EST stay different times, and opening either one starts the booking at that instant. A spring-forward day has no 2:00 AM. A visit on the board, and the move panel, names the start and end with the abbreviation, and both abbreviations when the visit crosses the change. Tour departures in the new-booking panel are that location’s local day, from local midnight until the next local midnight, so 11:30 PM stays on a 25-hour day and 12:30 AM the next morning stays off a 23-hour day. A location zone that is not a real name uses the business zone. Another workspace’s departure is not listed. The price, payments, taxes, fees, and refunds stay as they were.
