- The staff calendar puts each booking and departure on the date of its start in that location’s time zone. Choosing a location uses that zone for the day, week, month, and agenda, and for Today. With every location showing, each visit stays on its own location’s date. A location stored as UTC stays on the UTC date. A zone that is not a real name uses the business zone. A visit that crosses a daylight-saving change stays on the date it starts. Another workspace’s visit is not listed. The price, payments, taxes, fees, and refunds stay as they were.
