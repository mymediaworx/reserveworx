- A platform administrator’s workspace export includes the records added after the first export list: closures, the shared fleet and the units held back at a location, saved booking views, the setup guide, customer export and deletion rows, feedback, daily report summaries, and outbound webhook endpoints, deliveries, and tries. Another workspace’s rows are not in the file. A webhook signing secret, a customer sign-in token, a staff search token, and an undo row are left out. Passwords stay out. The export does not write a price, a payment, a tax, a fee, or a refund. Spec Section 12.
