- The dispatch manifest names each visit’s start and end in that location’s timezone, with the abbreviation. A visit that crosses a daylight-saving change names both, such as Sat, Mar 7, 10:00 PM EST – Sun, Mar 8, 4:00 AM EDT. On the fall-back morning, 1:30 AM EDT and 1:30 AM EST stay distinct. A departure uses the same clock. The location zone wins over the business zone. A location stored as UTC still shows UTC. A location zone that is not a real name uses the business zone. Another workspace’s booking is not listed. The CSV clock includes that abbreviation. Opening the page does not write a price rule, a payment, a tax, a fee, or a refund. Stored instants stay UTC.
