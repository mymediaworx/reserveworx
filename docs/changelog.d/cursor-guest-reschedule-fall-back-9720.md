- A guest reschedule keeps both hours on a fall-back morning. A plain start is the earlier hour. The later hour uses its offset, such as 01:30-05:00, and a suggested time posts that same offset. A time that does not exist, such as 2:30 AM on a spring-forward morning, is refused and the booking stays. A location zone that is not a real name uses the business zone. The price, payments, taxes, fees, and refunds stay as they were. Spec Section 2.4.
